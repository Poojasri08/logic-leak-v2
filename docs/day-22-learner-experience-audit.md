Day 22: Learner Experience Audit



Objective



Evaluate the Logic Leak learner journey from the perspective of a new learner and identify any usability gaps before moving further into productization.



Learner Journey Tested



Login → Challenge → Find the Leak → Explain → Fix → Earn XP → Track Progress → Next Challenge



Audit Findings



1\. Login and Challenge Flow



The existing login and challenge flow was reviewed and tested.



The learner can access the challenge and follow the intended progression without major confusion.



Status: Working as expected.



2\. Challenge Progression



The challenge progression from identifying the vulnerability to explaining and fixing it was reviewed.



The Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 progression provides a clear learning path and reward structure.



Status: Working as expected.



3\. XP and Progress Tracking



The XP and progress flow was reviewed after completing challenge activities.



The learner can understand their progress and continue to the next available challenge.



Status: Working as expected.



4\. Challenge Count



The current system contains three challenges.



For the current MVP validation stage, three challenges are sufficient to validate the complete learner journey and progression system.



More challenges can be added later as the learning content expands.



Status: Sufficient for current validation.



Product Gaps Identified



Signup



The current application does not provide a dedicated signup flow.



Adding signup would make it easier for new learners to create their own accounts and establish a complete onboarding experience.



Priority: High



Hints



A hint system could help learners who are stuck without immediately revealing the solution.



A possible future structure is:



\* Hint 1: Conceptual guidance

\* Hint 2: Stronger direction

\* Hint 3: Near-solution guidance



Hints should support learning rather than simply provide the answer.



Priority: Future enhancement



Overall Result



The core learner journey is functioning as intended.



No major usability problems were identified during this audit.



The main productization opportunities identified are:



1\. Add a signup flow.

2\. Introduce a progressive hint system.

3\. Expand the challenge library after the core product experience is established.



Day 22 Conclusion



Day 22 focused on validating the learner experience rather than adding new functionality.



The audit confirmed that the existing foundation is suitable for continued productization, while also identifying the next areas of improvement.



Foundation validated → Learner experience audited → Product gaps identified → Productization continues
